# Checklist Operator Polish Authority

**Phase:** `PHASE_CHECKLIST_07_OPERATOR_POLISH`  
**Status:** ACTIVE

## Authority Scope

CSS/UI polish for checklist operator ergonomics and visual hierarchy.

## Allowed Changes

- Spacing/density/hover/focus polish
- Minor label and action alignment polish
- Subtle transition tuning (non-disruptive)
- Diagnostics and governance artifacts

## Forbidden Changes

- No business logic change
- No persistence change
- No schema change
- No workflow or deep-link runtime contract changes
- No replacement of prior checklist UX contracts

## Completion Requirements

- Focused row remains clear.
- Dimmed rows remain readable/clickable.
- Progress/comment/copy-link/interaction signals remain visible.
- Keyboard focus and disabled states are clear.
